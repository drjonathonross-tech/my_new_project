Our approach

Chiropractic care rooted in how you move, think, and recover

Pain and dysfunction are often signals — not the whole story. Dr. Jonathon Ross combines chiropractic care with functional neurology and functional movement principles to identify what's driving your symptoms and address the underlying patterns holding you back.

The goal isn't just to feel better for a week. It's to restore the way your nervous system coordinates movement, balance, and adaptation — so you can stay active, resilient, and independent for the long run.

Functional neurology

Targeted assessment of brain-body integration — balance, coordination, eye movement, and autonomic function — to support nervous system recovery and performance.

Functional movement

Movement patterns are evaluated and retrained so your body handles daily demands — from workouts to desk work — with greater efficiency and less compensation.

Longevity-focused care

Treatment plans designed to protect mobility, neurological health, and physical capacity as you age — not just manage today's complaint.

BrainSPARQ

A brain-based framework for performance and resilience

BrainSPARQ is Dr. Jonathon Ross's approach to measuring and training how your brain and body work together — so care is guided by function, not guesswork.

Whether you are recovering from injury, chasing sport performance, or investing in long-term brain-body health, BrainSPARQ helps identify where your nervous system is strong, where it is compensating, and what to train next.

S

Speed

How quickly your nervous system processes information and initiates movement.

P

Power

The quality and force of coordinated motor output when your system is under demand.

A

Agility

Your ability to adapt — shifting strategies, directions, and states without breaking down.

R

Reaction

Timing, balance, and response accuracy when the environment changes in real time.

Q

Quickness

How efficiently you recover, reorganize, and return to a steady, high-performing baseline.
