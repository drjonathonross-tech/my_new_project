Who we are

Dr. Jonathon Ross & Dr. Elisa Petricca

Modern Family Wellness is led by us — partners in life and in work — bringing our clinical backgrounds and our experience as parents to families navigating the beautiful chaos of home life. We saw how often wellness advice felt overwhelming, clinical, or disconnected from what happens at the dinner table, in the car line, or during a bedtime meltdown.

On Instagram, we share nervous system regulation tools, simple calming strategies, sensory supports for emotional kids, and non-toxic home swaps — alongside chiropractic education focused on perinatal and paediatric care. We also highlight wellness brands we genuinely use and trust, because parents deserve honest recommendations, not another perfect routine.

Co-founder · Chiropractor
