BrainSPARQ

How I measure your brain-body function

BrainSPARQ is my framework for understanding how your brain and body are working together. Instead of guessing, we measure—so your care is based on what your nervous system actually needs.

Whether you're recovering from injury, improving your athletic performance, or investing in long-term health, BrainSPARQ assessment helps us identify where your nervous system is strong, where it's compensating, and exactly what to work on next.

S

Speed

How quickly your nervous system processes information and initiates movement.

P

Power

The quality and force of coordinated motor output when your system is under demand.

A

Agility

Your ability to adapt — shifting strategies, directions, and states without breaking down.

R

Reaction

Timing, balance, and response accuracy when the environment changes in real time.

Q

Quickness

How efficiently you recover, reorganize, and return to a steady, high-performing baseline.
